Using XREAL Glasses with a MagSafe Wrist Mount

A simple setup I've been trying for walking, chores, and watching videos in Side View mode.

I've been testing a simple MagSafe wrist mount with my XREAL glasses. The idea is straightforward: instead of keeping the phone in my pocket or holding it in my hand, I keep it attached to my wrist.

This works nicely when I use the glasses in Side View mode. I can walk, do small tasks around the house, or watch videos without constantly taking the phone in and out of my pocket.

It is not a perfect setup. The phone is still on your wrist, so you are aware of it. But compared with holding the phone in one hand, it feels much more natural for quick interactions.

The biggest benefit for me is that my hands are free most of the time. If I need to tap something, pause a video, reply quickly, or adjust something, the phone is already there. Then I can immediately go back to whatever I was doing.

Another thing I like is not having to pull the phone out of my pocket every few minutes. It sounds minor, but when using AR glasses, it makes the whole experience feel smoother.
